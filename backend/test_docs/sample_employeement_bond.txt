This Employment Bond ("Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of October 2025 at Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

BETWEEN:
Apex Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at SG Highway, Ahmedabad (hereinafter referred to as the "Company").

AND:
Mr. Rahul Sharma, son of Mr. Rajesh Sharma, residing at Flat 402, Green Park Apartments, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad (hereinafter referred to as the "Employee").

CLAUSE 1: OBLIGATION & SERVICE PERIOD
1.1 The Employee agrees to serve the Company for a minimum continuous period of twenty-four (24) months starting from 1st October 2025.
1.2 The Employee acknowledges that the Company will incur substantial expenses exceeding INR 1,500,000 for specialized training in Software Development during the initial 3 months.

2.2 The Employee agrees that this amount is a reasonable pre-estimate of loss under Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

CLAUSE 3: JURISDICTION
3.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
3.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Courts at Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
